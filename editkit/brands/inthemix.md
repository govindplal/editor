# In the Mix Podcast

Platform & format: Instagram reels / YouTube shorts 9:16 promos of full YouTube episodes.   Language(s): Malayalam +
English mix (Manglish): captions in English, translating the Malayalam; ingest with `--lang ml`.
Logline: guest conversations (actors, sportspeople) in Kochi; reels tease the full episode on YouTube.

## Look
- Brand: acid yellow `#e4e62e` on black `#0d0d0c`, paper `#f6f3ea` (cue cards, logo card at the end of each source).
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque condensed (display/captions), Instrument Serif italic (accents, host lines), Martian Mono
  (kickers, credits); all in `editkit/hf/template.html`.
- Grade: guest warm skin, cool shadows; host softer and warm; a memory look for nostalgia; glow on laughs; the logo
  card stays neutral (`end`).
- Lane: premium-editorial with playful gags (stickman scenes, "NO. NO." stamps) on the funny beats.

## Captions
- Left-aligned, lower-left over the guest's shirt; dark plates over host shots (bright yellow cue card); seam band on
  split screens. Host lines in serif italic (tag "Host" when over guest footage).
- Names heard so far: Lijo Jose Pellissery, Bejoy Nambiar, Dinesh Mongia, Sachin.

## Graphics & pace
- Guest ident at frame 0 (kicker "In the Mix · Podcast", serif name, role).
- B-roll duotone photo cards for every named person; line-art illustrations for physical or funny anecdotes.
- End card: guest name + "Full episode on YouTube" CTA button with a cursor click, below the show's logo card.
